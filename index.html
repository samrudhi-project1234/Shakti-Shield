The Problem

Emergencies don't wait for perfect connectivity.

During an emergency, people may have limited internet, unreliable GPS, low battery or very little time to communicate. A safety platform needs to account for these real-world limitations.

📡

Connectivity Gaps

Internet-dependent communication may become unreliable when network availability is limited.

⏱

Every Second Matters

Users need a quick and discreet way to request assistance without navigating through multiple screens.

👁

Unclear Response

Guardians need clear information about location, battery, delivery and emergency status.

Our Solution

One safety layer. Multiple ways to stay connected.

Shakti Shield combines emergency activation, location sharing, adaptive communication and trusted-response coordination into one safety platform.

✓ Activate emergency assistance quickly.

How It Works

From SOS to trusted response.

A simple emergency flow designed around speed, communication reliability and clear response.

📍

Core Features

Built around the moments that matter.

Every feature is designed to make emergency activation, communication and response simpler.

⚡

Quick SOS

Trigger an emergency alert quickly from the home screen with a simple, accessible interface.

📍

Location Sharing

Share current or last-known location with authorized trusted contacts.

📡

Offline SMS Fallback

When internet connectivity is unavailable, the system can use SMS fallback or queue the alert.

👥

Trusted Contacts

Choose parents, friends, family members or other trusted people to receive alerts.

🔋

Battery Awareness

Monitor battery status and reduce tracking frequency when battery becomes low.

🔒

Emergency Lock

Keep the emergency session active until the user confirms that they are safe.

🔔

Delivery Status

Provide visibility into alert delivery and response status.

🗺

Nearby Support

Help identify nearby approved support points such as hospitals, police stations and campus security.
